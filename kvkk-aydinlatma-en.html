3. Transfer of Personal Data

Your data is transferred to service providers whose servers are located abroad (Supabase, RevenueCat, Apple, Resend) for the provision of hosting, authentication, subscription management, notification delivery and e-mail delivery services. Your user account identifier (user ID) is sent to RevenueCat so that your subscription status can be matched to your account. If you have granted notification permission, your notification token is processed through Apple's notification infrastructure (APNs) for the delivery of notifications. Your e-mail address and name are sent to our e-mail delivery provider Resend so that you can be e-mailed in cases such as the approval of your cove suggestion.

International transfers are carried out under KVKK Article 9: as no adequacy decision has been issued by the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board for the countries where these service providers are located, the transfer relies on the explicit consent obtained during sign-up. Data processed in flows that do not require an account (crowd reports, business/change requests) is either processed without directly identifying information (crowd reports; as explained above, indirect matching via the device identifier is possible on the server side) or transferred to these servers on the basis of the explicit consent you gave by submitting the relevant form. If you do not give explicit consent, features requiring an account cannot be used; you may continue to benefit from the parts of the App that can be used without an account.

4. Method of Collection

Your personal data is collected by automated means during the sign-up and usage processes of the App, within the scope of the permissions granted by your device (location service, notification permission, photo library access) and through notifications received from purchase infrastructures.

6. Your Rights Under KVKK Article 11

  • To learn whether your personal data is processed and to request information in that regard,
  • To learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in accordance with that purpose,
  • To know the third parties to whom the data is transferred, in Türkiye or abroad,
  • To request correction if the data is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • To request deletion or destruction of the data under KVKK Article 7,
  • To request that correction/deletion operations be notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred,
  • To object to a result arising against you through analysis made exclusively by automated systems,
  • To claim compensation for damages arising from unlawful processing.

7. Applications

You can submit requests regarding your rights, in accordance with the Turkish Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller, through the following methods:

  • E-mail (application/notification address): The contact address designated by us for applications is emre@pimrobotics.com; you can send your application to this address using the e-mail address registered in our system (you can also reach it via the ready template under Profile > Privacy within the App),
  • Other methods: You may also apply via secure electronic signature, mobile signature or registered electronic mail (KEP).

Your application is concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest, depending on its nature.
